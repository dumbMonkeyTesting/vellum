02 · Watchlist

Keep a quiet eye.

Saved only in this browser. A SOL balance is read when you ask. No prices.

    New launches, as they land.

    The newest pump.fun launches this reader has seen. A listing is not an endorsement. Names are not unique: check the CA.

    Watch a name.

    Type a ticker or a name. Vellum lists pump.fun launches it has seen, and older pump pairs whose name or ticker matches exactly. A coin is then saved. It does not stop a copy from launching.

    Same deployer.

    Scan the recent transactions of a wallet for a pump CA, or save the wallet and Vellum will note the next deploy it sees from that address (since Vellum was deployed).
